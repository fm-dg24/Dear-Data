## Design plan:
### Initial design
Represent the sound using 100 fixed spaced out shapes, have each sound represent different categories by the different geometry of each shape, and loudness by size of each shape. The colour of each shape can represent the location of the sound (indoor or outdoor).

### Second design
Have all 100 sounds represented as lines forming the shape of a sound wave. Have each portion of the wave be a line where each line represents 1 data item. The sound will continuously loop from left to right to create a visual of the sound wave moving. There will be buttons that the viewer can press that will change the lines on the waves colour based on their category, either sound type or location.

### Third design
The third design is a radial sound wave, where the sound data is represented by the colour and length of lines that are placed on the outer perimeter of a circle in the middle of the canvas. The cicrle will rotate 360 degrees and loop to continuously have slow endless rotations. 

### Fourth design
The fourth design is the same ad the second except the peak of each sound data is connected with a thin faint line that shows clearly how the data varies from the first to the last observation, and the wave will be infront of a dark background to make the colours of the wave stand out more.

### First draft design choice
For the first draft I decided to go with the lines forming the shape of a sound wave, however right now there is no feature to change the colour based on sound type or location, and the wave does not move.

### Second draft
The second draft is the same as the 1st except and colours are used to show the type of location of each individual sound data, and Pressing T changes the visualisation to show type and pressing L changes the visualisation to show location. The wave does move but it does not loop left to right as expected making it look less like a wave and more like a graph.

### Third draft
The third draft adds a legend to associate the colour with their sound type or location depending on what has been selected.
